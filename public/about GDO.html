About Me

Hello there. My name is Glenn Duero Odtojan. I was born last May 22, 1981 at Bislig City, Surigao del Sur, Philippines. I'm presently living here in Butuan City since 2016, with my wife, Ivy Joy T. Odtojan. My occupation is an employee at a government agency where I hold a position of Engineer II.

I recieved my Bachelors degree in Agricultural Engineering last 2007 at Xavier University in Cagayan de oro City. As my first job, I worked as a Contractual Engineer at Bislig City Local Government Unit. After two years, I transferred to Philippine Rice Research Institute (PhilRice), working as a Technology Extension Worker. When my contract with PhilRice ended, I became an Overseas Filipino Worker (OFW) in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. I worked as a Public Health Inspector at King Abdul Aziz Medical City, Riyadh, where my contract lasted for three years.

When I came back to the Philippines, I got hired by a government agency here in Butuan City as a contractual, and eventually became a regular employee since 2020. Currently, my job as a government employee is the longest time I worked for a company or agency. I have been working there since 2017 and hopefully will continue until retirement.

My hobbies include playing video games and fishkeeping. The games I play are offline single player RPGs, like Skyrim, The Witcher III, and Fallout. As a fishkeeper, I maintain several tanks including 2 30-gallons, 2 20-gallons, 2 15-gallons and 4 smaller tanks. My favorite fish to keep are Corydoras catfish because they are adorable and easy to keep. I provided some information on my website on how to keep and breed them.
