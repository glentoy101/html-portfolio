THE BEST MOVIES FOR ME

My Top 3 Movies of all-time

I like the storyline of the movie. The hero having amnesia and trying very hard
to learn his true identity. The Fight scenes are exciting.

Top 2: Venom

Action packed movie with wild scenes and funny dialogs

Top 3: Saving Private Ryan

Epic War movie with realistic performances. This film hits me deep.
